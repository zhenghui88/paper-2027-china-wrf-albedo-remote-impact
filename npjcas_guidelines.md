# npj CAS (npj Climate and Atmospheric Science) Submission Guidelines

## Aims & Scope

npj Climate and Atmospheric Science considers research that explores all aspects of the physical, chemical, and biological components of the climate and atmospheric sciences.

The journal covers a broad range of topics including but not limited to:

- Climate dynamics and variability
- Weather forecasting and climate prediction
- Climate change and paleoclimate, including cryosphere
- Ocean dynamics
- Dynamical meteorology
- Weather extremes
- Air pollution
- Atmospheric chemistry and physics, including aerosols
- The hydrological cycle and atmosphere-ocean and atmosphere-land interactions, including vegetation
- Use of advanced numerical methods, such as machine learning and artificial intelligence, applied to weather, climate, and air quality prediction

## Formatting requirements

title page: A title that is up to 15 words in length and free of punctuation, idioms, and puns; the full author list; author affiliation information, including institution, city, and country; and the corresponding author(s) email address

abstract: No subheadings permitted and up to 150 words in length (not mandatory)

introduction: No subheadings permitted

results: Subheadings should be used

discussion: No subheadings, limitations, or conclusions sections are permitted

methods: Subheadings should be used. All descriptions of Methods should be provided in the main manuscript file and not in the Supplementary Information

data availability: Mandatory

code availability: Where applicable

acknowledgement: Where Funding for the study should be declared, a separate Funding statement is not permitted

author contributions: Mandatory, the statement should refer to all authors individually, denoted by their initials

competing interests: Mandatory, you should declare all financial and non-financial competing interests for each author. If there are no competing interests to declare, this should be stated

references: Limited to 60 references, though not strictly enforced

figure legends: Limited to 350 words per figure. Multi-panel figures should be presented on a single page with each panel labeled using an a), b), c) convention.

Supplementary information files: permitted but will not be editted by the journal. combined into a single, separate file, preferably in PDF format.
