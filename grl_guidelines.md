# GRL (Geophysical Research Letters) Submission Guidelines

> **Official Scope & Limits:** Research letters are articles on major advances in all major geoscience disciplines. Letters should have broad and immediate implications in their discipline or across the geosciences. Research letters are limited to 12 publication units. Submitted manuscripts longer than 12 publication units will be returned for shortening.

## Target Journal Strategy

- **Target:** GRL (Geophysical Research Letters).
- **Focus:** The main text must focus purely on the *geophysical discovery*.

## Formatting & Length Constraints

- **Length Constraint:** GRL limits are strict (<12 publication units, 1 publication unit = 1 figure = 1 table = 500 words).
- **Action Required:** Move detailed mathematical formulations and optimizer settings to Supporting Information (`si.md`).

## AGU Content Guidelines (GRL Specific)

### Abstract

- **Content:** Introduce topic -> Explain knowledge gap -> Brief methods/data -> Key results -> Novelty.
- **Audience:** Discipline experts + accessible to broader scientific community.
- **Constraints:**
  - Single paragraph.
  - < 150 words (Strict GRL limit).

### Plain Language Summary (PLS)

- **Content:** Same as Abstract (Study summary, results, broader relevance) but distinct tone.
- **Tone:** No jargon. Completely different language.
- **Audience:** Non-discipline scientists, journalists, educators.
- **Constraints:**
  - Single paragraph.
  - < 200 words.

### Key Points

- **Constraints:** Up to 3 key points, must not exceed 140 characters each.
